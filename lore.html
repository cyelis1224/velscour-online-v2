Chapter I · The Abyssal Foundation

The Thing Beneath the Hollow

"A world built upon something that should have stayed dead." — Inscription on the Warded Well of Old Herrington

Five hundred strata beneath the roots of the ancient oaks lies a throne of black iron that no mortal mason ever raised. Upon it sits Demon Lord Velscour — not slain in the First Crusade, as the court chroniclers once comforted themselves by writing, merely pinned beneath the weight of five hundred sealed depths, four elemental ward-crystals, and four obelisks of chained basalt. For a thousand years his heart has beaten out of time with the living world, and every century that pulse climbs higher through the stone.

In Herrington Hollow, the elders remember when the dungeon entrance was nothing more than a drystone well on the edge of the trees. Now the well water tastes of cold iron, the hens stare unblinking toward the glowing portal until dawn, and the buried dead do not wait for Depth Nine to walk — they claw out of the ossuary niches at Depth One. Every fifth floor downward has been claimed by one of Velscour's forty-eight Raid Tyrants, sealing its archway behind iron grates until either challenger or sovereign falls.

Chapter II · The Last Sanctuaries

Herrington Hollow & Sunscorch Oasis

"The Order keeps the town. You keep the dark at bay." — Knightly Salute of the Ten Surface Orders

Above the portal stands Herrington Hollow, a lantern-lit refuge of mud lanes, market stalls, and stubbornly smoking chimneys watched over by King Edric, Queen Elowen, young royal heir Pip, and the indomitable Dowager Queen Agnes — who famously buried two kings and a dragon and still wears steel beneath her mourning velvet. Ten sworn Knightly Orders — from Aldric of the Ashen Bastion and Elsabet of the Ivory Mantle to Halvard the Frostbound and Odo of the Hollow Star — hold the timber palisades and forest roads so the townsfolk can bake bread, mend wagon wheels, and haggle with Fortunato while lone champions brave the descent.

Those who conquer the Bone Colossus at Depth 96 break through the subterranean crust into a second sun-bleached world: Sunscorch Oasis, the desert sanctuary of Act II. Framed by date palms, canvas pavilions, and wind-scoured sandstone, the Oasis shelters travelers before they march into the burning Sea of Sand, the spiral stairs of the Serpentine Tower, and the buried tombs of the Sunless Dynasty.

Chapter III · Sacred Arms & Lost Crusades

The High Reliquary, Covenants & Old Herrington

"Nine sovereigns wore it into the catacombs. Nine sovereigns fell." — Reliquary Record for The Ninefold Crown

Long before the current watch, the High Reliquary marshalled great crusades into the catacombs, arming its champions with named Heirlooms and binding whole brotherhoods under five-piece Covenant oaths. Their warhammers, censers, and shields still surface in the deep chests — scarred links that remember vows kept unto death, petrified abyssal lenses that never blink, and the battered iron plate of Old Herrington, forged when the first wall held the eastern breach until sunrise.

Older still are the twenty Sigil Stones — from humble Ash, Bryn, and Cael to royal Syl and Thane. When set in exact ancient sequence inside unadorned grey steel, these stones awaken living Glyphwords such as Mourning Bell, Ember Litany, Last Light, Sky-Sunder, and the forbidden six-stone Abyssal Writ.

Chapter IV · The Fractured Horizons

Beyond the Crypts: The Sea of Sand, The Sky Lanes & The Eternal Breach

"That smell is not the sea air. That is a crypt. Up here." — Boarding Log of the Ironclad, Sky Lanes Chronicle I

Velscour's corruption is no mere tunnel beneath one village; the descent opens onto distant, fractured horizons bound to his dominion. Beneath the Scorched Wastes rules Pharaoh Sekhmun, the Exchanged King, who bartered his dynasty's beating hearts for an eternal reign of gold-bound sarcophagi and scarab carpets. On the storm-lashed Pirate Seas, the Lampwardens' vessel Dawnwake trades cannon fire with the undead Black Tide; high above the Empyrean clouds, ironclad skyships clash across thirty storied boarding campaigns against Admiral Veyl's black-sail privateers, Morvane's Widow's Bell, and Queen Hiemra's floating ice palace.

Back in Herrington Hollow, a second portal burns violet beside the green treeline: The Eternal Breach. Stepping through it casts heroes onto fifty unmoored islands of the Veilscar March drifting over raw void, hunted by two hundred void-trimmed aberrations and the Seven Elemental Wyrms, whose sevenfold ascension culminates at Floor 50 in the sixty-segment Apex Ethereal Wyrm.

Chapter V · Beyond Depth 500

The Shattering: Heresies, Abyssal Rifts & Velscour Unbound

"He wore it for a thousand years. It has not decided whether you are the next." — Crown of the Demon Lord (Unbound Heirloom)

Striking down Demon Lord Velscour at Depth 500 does not end the war — it breaks the ancient lock that kept the deeper abyss asleep. In Herrington Hollow, the Altar of Heresy kindles with twelve profane vows: swearing them doubles monster packs, dries every potion flask, calls Pit Legions through sudden hellgates, and pushes Raid Lords into a lethal Fifth Phase in exchange for immense treasure multipliers and Infernal Marks coveted by the ash-cloaked wanderer Malakor.

Simultaneously, Abyssal Rifts tear open across a hundred escalating tiers where Rift Guardians hoard Abyssal Keys. Carrying those keys back to the Town Portal unlocks Unbound Exploration — a relentless hundred-floor hardmode plunge culminating before Velscour Unbound. Freed of his ancient chains, the Archfiend summons two Signature Raid Tyrants of his Court of the Abyss to shield him under a blood pact, challenging mortal legends for the five Unbound Heirlooms of the Pit.

👑 Depth 500 Throne Climax 🔥 Fire / Shadow / Abyssal Unbound Rematch: Floor 100

DEMON LORD VELSCOUR

Ancient Archfiend of the Sunken Throne · Master of the Legion of the Pit

Seated high upon a basalt dais at Depth 500, Velscour commands a five-phase, fifteen-minute endurance war with life-gated intermissions. In Velscour Unbound (20:00 clock), two Signature Raid Lords of his Court of the Abyss fight beside him, granting him a 60% damage-reduction Pact while they live.

Phase I · Lord Upon the Throne (100–80%) Hellbrand cleaves, Chains of Velscour drag heroes inward, and Throne Guards march.
Phase II · The Four Seals (80% Gate) Invulnerable intermission: shatter 4 Seal Wardens amid sweeping flame walls (+15% boss power per unbroken seal).
Phase III · Crown of Hellfire (80–50%) Creeping lava tides, Meteor Spirals, Doom Brand pools, Mirror of Sin clones & Pit Hellgates.
Phase IV · Into the Abyss (50% Gate) Pitch darkness falls for 75s: hug the two Soul Lanterns while shattering three Heart Shards (-5% boss HP each).
Phase V · True Form & Obelisks (50–0%) Arena-wide Apocalypse blasts (3 safe sigils); at 10% HP he chains himself to 4 Unbound Obelisks.
