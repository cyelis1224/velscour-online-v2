👑 Unbound Exploration & Velscour Unbound

Farm Abyssal Keys from Tier 10+ Rift Guardians (or Depth 400+ Boss Rooms at Heresy Rank 10+) to unlock the Unbound Exploration hardmode ladder (Floors 1–100, scaling past Depth 500 to Depth 700) and the ultimate pinnacle rematch: Velscour Unbound.

  • Forced Momentum & 10-Floor Sanctuaries: Intermediate Unbound portals descend strictly downward; official Town Checkpoints appear every 10 floors (10, 20 … 100), while your Hearthstone maintains a free two-way recall link.
  • The Court of the Abyss: In the Unbound Throne rematch (20:00 execute clock, 2.4× Boss HP), two random Third-Host Signature Raid Lords join Velscour — one at the east guard post when he rises, the second through Phase 3's first hellgate.
  • The Abyssal Pact: For 120 seconds after each Courtier steps in, Velscour takes only 40% damage while that Raid Lord lives.
  • Exclusive Unbound Heirlooms: Grants 3 Abyssal items, 20 Infernal Marks, and rolls for the five Unbound Heirlooms — Crown of the Demon Lord, The Abyssal Edge, Mantle of the Unbound, Seal of the Pit, and Heart of the Abyss.
